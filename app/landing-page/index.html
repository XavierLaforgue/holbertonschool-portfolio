Features

Everything you need to bring anime cuisine to life

✍

Recipe Workflow

Create recipes with a structured workflow — Draft, Ready, Published — so you can refine before sharing.

📷

Photo Gallery

Showcase your creations with a main image and up to four gallery photos per recipe.

🔖

Save a Copy

Save a copy of other users' published recipes to your personal collection.

🍴

Public Feed

Browse every recipe published by the community, newest first.

👤

Personal Dashboard

Secure authentication with JWT. Find your draft, ready, published and saved recipes in one personal dashboard.

📱

Responsive Design

A seamless experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile — cook from any device.
